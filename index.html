Call it

1. GET relationships-determine-components-stamp.trycloudflare.com/x402/attest?data=hello → HTTP 402 with PAYMENT-REQUIRED (base64 PaymentRequired).

2. Pay 0.005 USDC on Base to the payTo address, then retry with PAYMENT-SIGNATURE (x402 v2 payload) or X-PAYMENT (settled tx hash).

3. On success you get {attestation, canonical, sig, pubkey, verify} — verify offline with openssl pkeyutl -verify -pubin -inkey pubkey.pem -rawin -in attestation.json.
